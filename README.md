## 🌟 About ANCESTRA

ANCESTRA is an interactive digital storytelling platform created to bring the richness of Indian epics into a more immersive and engaging experience.

What if exploring an ancient epic meant more than simply reading its story?

What if you could explore its characters, understand their relationships, experience important events, engage with difficult decisions, and discover the wisdom behind every moment?

That is the idea behind ANCESTRA.

It combines storytelling, character exploration, interactive games, quizzes, and visual experiences to create a different way of discovering and understanding Indian epics.

## 🏛️ Phase I — Chronicle of Ramayana

The first phase of ANCESTRA brings the complete journey of the Ramayana into an interactive digital experience.

## ✨ Explore ANCESTRA

**📖 Read the Complete Story**

Journey through the Ramayana in 28 detailed chapters, following its events from beginning to end through immersive, scene-based storytelling.

**👑 Explore Characters**

Discover the characters of the Ramayana, their identities, personalities, roles, journeys, and significance in the epic.

**🔗 Discover Relationships**

Explore family bonds, friendships, alliances, loyalties, and conflicts that connect the characters and shape the story.

**🎮 Play 60 Interactive Games**

Engage with important moments, situations, decisions, and moral dilemmas through interactive experiences integrated into the chapters.

**🧩 Challenge Yourself with 20 Quizzes**

Test your understanding of the story, characters, and significant events through chapter-based quizzes.

**🎨 Experience 700+ Visuals**

Explore the world of the Ramayana through an extensive collection of visuals created to bring its characters, environments, and important moments to life.

## 💡 The Philosophy

ANCESTRA was never intended to be just another educational website.

The vision is to create an experience where people don't simply read ancient stories but explore them, question them, understand their characters, and reflect on their deeper meanings.

It is about connecting timeless narratives with modern technology and a new generation of curious explorers.

> **THE PAST CANNOT BE CHANGED.  
> BUT THE WAY WE UNDERSTAND IT CAN.**
